Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

PuruPop ("we", "the Game") is designed to collect as little data as possible. This page explains exactly what is, and isn't, collected today, and what would change if optional features below are switched on.

What we store on your device

Your level, coin balance, power-up counts, and sound/music preferences are saved using your browser's localStorage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device — it is not sent to any server.
  • Is not linked to your identity, an account, or an email address (the Game has no account system).
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Advertising

The Game offers optional rewarded video ads: you may choose to watch a short video to earn a power-up. Watching is always voluntary — you can decline and keep playing.

  • In the browser version, the rewarded-ad flow is simulated entirely on your device. No ad network is contacted and no advertising data is collected.
  • In native app builds, ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), IP address, and general ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads. This data is handled under Google's Privacy & Terms.

Where required by law, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you may choose non-personalized ads instead. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time through your device's settings.

Purchases

The Game awards in-game coins for clearing levels. Spending those coins is not yet available ("Coming soon"), and no real-money purchases are currently offered. We therefore collect no payment information of any kind. If purchases launch in the future, payment processing will be handled by a third-party payment provider (or the relevant app store) and this policy will be updated to describe that before the change ships.

Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for a general audience, including children, and collects no personal information directly from anyone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Where the audience may include children, advertising is configured to request non-personalized/contextual ads only, consistent with applicable regulations such as COPPA, the GDPR, and Japan's APPI.

Your rights and choices

  • Local data: clear all locally-stored progress at any time via your browser's or device's site/app settings.
  • Advertising: reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings.
  • GDPR (EEA/UK): where applicable, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

Because the Game has no account system, requests relating to specific data may require you to identify the data in question. Contact us using the address below.
